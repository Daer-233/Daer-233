# CrossBorder AgentHub Privacy Policy / 隐私声明

**Operator / 运营者** Daer  
**Last updated / 最近更新** 2026-11-23

## How we handle data

Daer operates CrossBorder AgentHub, an e-commerce product research and selection workspace. This policy covers the workspace, its optional Amazon Selling Partner API integration, and this public notice.


### Data we process and why


- **Workspace data.** We process account and role information, sign-in records, task inputs, and related audit records to provide access, maintain workflows, and investigate misuse. A session cookie keeps users signed in.
- **Amazon product research.** When an authorized seller enables the integration, the application uses Amazon SP-API to read US product catalog information, such as identifiers, titles, and dimensions, and may read item offers if pricing access is enabled. We use the information to compare products and prices for that seller. This integration requests catalog and pricing data and does not request buyer identities, contact details, or payment information.


### Access and sharing

Amazon research evidence is available to users authorized for the associated store and task. The application uses server-side credentials for Amazon authentication and keeps them out of user reports. Amazon research evidence and credentials are excluded from external AI model prompts. Optional workspace AI features may send the task input and permitted workspace context to the model provider configured in that deployment. We restrict access to the data necessary to operate and secure these functions. GitHub hosts this privacy page and may process visitor information as described in its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).


### Retention and deletion

The workspace retains Amazon research evidence for 24 hours by default, or for a shorter configured period. Disabling the connection in CrossBorder removes its saved Amazon research evidence and stops new requests. Sellers can also revoke app authorization in Amazon. Workspace account and audit records are retained as needed for operation, security, and applicable obligations.

## 隐私声明

Daer 运营 CrossBorder AgentHub 电商调研与选品工作台。本声明适用于工作台、可选的 Amazon Selling Partner API 接入和本公开页面。


### 处理的数据与用途


- **工作台数据。**我们处理账号与角色信息、登录记录、任务输入和相关操作记录，用于访问控制、业务流程和安全排查。工作台使用会话 Cookie 保持登录。
- **Amazon 商品调研。**卖家授权并开启服务后，应用通过 Amazon SP-API 读取美国站商品目录，包括商品编号、标题和尺寸。开启报价权限时，也可读取商品报价。数据用于该卖家的商品及价格比较。此接入请求商品目录和报价数据，不请求买家身份、联系方式或付款信息。


### 访问与共享

Amazon 调研证据仅对获授权的店铺及任务用户可见。Amazon 凭证在服务器端用于认证，不出现在业务报告中。Amazon 调研证据和凭证不进入外部 AI 模型的提示。使用可选 AI 功能时，任务输入与获准的工作台上下文可能发送给部署所配置的模型服务。我们将访问范围限定在运行和保护这些功能所需的数据。此隐私页由 GitHub 托管，GitHub 对访客信息的处理见其[隐私声明](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)。


### 保留与删除

Amazon 调研证据默认保留 24 小时，可配置为更短期限。在 CrossBorder 中停用连接时，系统删除已保存的 Amazon 调研证据并停止新请求。卖家也可以在 Amazon 撤销应用授权。工作台账号与操作记录按运行、安全和适用义务所需期限保留。
